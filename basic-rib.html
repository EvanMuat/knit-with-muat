Basic 2x2 Rib Pattern

Materials Needed:

  • Yarn (any weight)
  • Knitting needles (size appropriate for yarn)

Instructions:


  1. Cast on a multiple of 4 stitches +2

  2. Row 1: *K2, P2; repeat from * to last 2 stitches, P2.

  3. Row 2: *P2, K2; repeat from * to last 2 stitches, K2.

  4. Repeat Rows 1 and 2 until the piece reaches the desired length.

  5. Cast off.

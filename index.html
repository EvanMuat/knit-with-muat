Why Learn to Knit?

Provides a creative outlet

Knitting allows you to express your creativity through colors, textures, and patterns.

Helps with memory retention

learning patterns keeps the brain active and can lower the risk of cognitive decline.

Improves fine motor skills

Working with knitting needles and yarn can help improve hand-eye coordination

Great for stress relief

The rhythmic nature of knitting can be calming and help reduce stress and anxiety.

Brilliant social activity

There are lots of knitting groups where you can meet new people and strengthen comunity bonds

Improves problem solving skills

Navigating complex patterns can enhance critical thinking and problem-solving abilities.

So why not start your knitting journey today?

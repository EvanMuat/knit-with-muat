Basic Cable Pattern

Materials Needed:

  • Yarn (any weight)
  • Knitting needles (size appropriate for yarn)
  • Cable needle (same size as knitting needles)

Starter Rows: (theese rows don't repeat.)


  1. Cast on a multiple of 11 stitches + 5

  2. Starter Row 1: *P5, K6, P5, repeat from * to end of row.
  3. Starter Row 2: *K5, P6, K5, repeat from * to end of row.

Repeating Rows:


  1. Row 1:*P5, K6, P5, repeat from * to end of row.

  2. Row 2:*K5, P6, K5, repeat from * to end of row.

  3. Row 3:*P5, K6, P5, repeat from * to end of row.

  4. Row 4:*K5, P6, K5, repeat from * to end of row.

  5. Row 5:*P5, K6, P5, repeat from * to end of row.

  6. Row 6:*K5, P6, K5, repeat from * to end of row.

  7. Row 7:*P5, K6, P5, repeat from * to end of row.

  8. Row 8:*K5, P6, K5, repeat from * to end of row.

  9. Row 9:P5, *sl. next 3 sts onto cable needle, leave at back, K3, K3 sts from cable needle, P5, repeat from * to end of row.

  10. Row 10:*K5, P6, K5, repeat from * to end of row.

  11. Repeat Rows 1-10 until the piece reaches the desired length.
  12. Cast off.
